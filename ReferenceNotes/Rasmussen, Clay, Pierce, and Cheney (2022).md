

Notes re: [Rasmussen, et al. (2022)](../References.md#rasmussen-clay-pierce-cheney-2022)

---

Early chapters cover basics of behavior analysis. Pretty much the standard stuff (updated from Skinner's original stuff). 

Nice to see that [Sidman (1960)](../References.md#sidman-1960) is referenced throughout with regard to experimental design/interpretation. Sidman's work has aged well over the past 5+ decades.

Discussion of "Delayed Conditioning" (pg. 89) evokes "behavioral discrepancy", as discussed in [Donahoe, Burgos, & Palmer (1993)](../References.md#donahoe-burgos-palmer-1993), section "Toward a Biobehavioral Principle of Reinforcement", which includes subsection "A Unified Principle of Reinforcement". This is the earliest article laying out details of their bio-plausible artificial neural network, which they call SelNet.

This prompted me to search the book to references to Donahoe's work. The only one is to a much later work (which I have not yet read). That is a very odd ommision for this 2022 book, which is titled "*Behavior Analysis and Learning: A Biobehavioral Approach"*.  [Donahoe & Palmer (1994) eds.](../References.md#donahoe-palmer-1994) is an entire book on behavior analysis from a biobehavioral perspective, and discussed a biobehavioral approach extensively in [Donahoe, Burgos, & Palmer (1993)](../References.md#donahoe-burgos-palmer-1993). Rasmussen, et al., seem to have skipped all of the foundational biobehavioral literature in behavior analysis. Why?
