# Annotations: Donahoe, Palmer, and Burgos (1993)

[Citation](../References.md#donahoe-palmer-burgos-1993)

---

1. **Probably the best introduction to the SelNet bio-plausible artificial neural network and its operation.**
2. Description of a "unified principle of reinforcement" (UPR) throughout.
3. Describes the responsible diffuse signals to/from VTA and hippocampus, and how simulated in SelNet.
4. General description with schematics of example networks. No experiment here, just description.
5. Describes several behavioral phenomena and procedures to illustrate the action on behavior of a neural net using dopaminergic and hippocampal signals as agents of selection.
	1. Acquisition: pp 30-32
	2. Extinction and reacquisition: pg 32
	3. Conditioned reinforcement:
		1. Acquired reinforcement: pp 25-26
		2. Simulation of Acquired Reinforcement": pp 32-33
	4. Stimulus-Control: pp 33-35
		1. Blocking: pg 33
		2. Discrimination: pp 33-34
		3. Stimulus-selection (section "Stimulus configuring"): pp 34-35
6. Learning and activation equations are described in the Appendix (pp 39-40). ***TOBEDONE: same as equations used in later years?***
